What Is an ATS Resume Checker?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software used by 95%+ of large companies and recruiters to automatically filter resumes before a human ever sees them. If your resume isn't ATS-compatible, it gets rejected regardless of your qualifications.

How to Pass ATS Screening

  • Use standard section headers: "Work Experience" not "Where I've Made an Impact." ATS looks for conventional headings.
  • Avoid tables and columns: Multi-column layouts and tables confuse ATS parsers. Use a single-column, top-to-bottom layout.
  • Include keywords from the job description: Mirror the exact skills, tools, and titles mentioned in the job posting.
  • Save as PDF or DOCX: These are the most ATS-compatible formats. Avoid images of text, Canva exports, or Google Docs links.
  • Keep contact info in the body: Don't put your name, email, or phone in headers or footers — many ATS systems skip those entirely.
  • Use standard fonts: Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman, or similar. Decorative fonts may not render or parse correctly.
  • Skip graphics and icons: Skill bars, star ratings, and icons are invisible to ATS. List skills as plain text instead.
  • Spell out acronyms: Write "Search Engine Optimization (SEO)" on first use. ATS may search for either form.
