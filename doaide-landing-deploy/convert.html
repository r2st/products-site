About

Why you need a free file converter online.

File format incompatibility is one of the most common digital friction points. You receive a PDF that needs editing, an image in the wrong format, or a spreadsheet that needs to be a CSV. Most file converters either put watermarks on the output, limit you to one or two free conversions per day, or require a subscription. DoAide Convert is a free file converter online that removes all of those barriers — convert as many files as you need, in any supported format, with no strings attached.

PDF to Word converter free

The most common file conversion need is PDF to Word. You receive a contract, a report, or a form as a PDF and need to edit it. DoAide Convert is a PDF to Word converter free that preserves formatting, tables, images, and text styles during conversion. The output is a clean DOCX file that opens in Microsoft Word or Google Docs, ready to edit. No watermark on the output, no limit on the number of conversions, and no account required.

Image format conversion

Different platforms require different image formats. A website needs WebP for speed, social media wants PNG for transparency, and a printer needs TIFF for high-resolution output. DoAide Convert handles all the common image conversions: PNG to JPG, JPG to PNG, PNG to WebP, SVG to PNG, GIF to MP4, and more. Bulk conversion lets you process multiple images at once, and you can adjust quality and dimensions during conversion.

Document merge and split

Beyond format conversion, DoAide Convert can merge multiple PDFs into a single document, split a large PDF into individual pages, and combine images into a single PDF. These operations are common in business workflows — combining invoices for accounting, splitting a scanned document into separate files, or creating a PDF portfolio from individual images. All of this is free and requires no login.

Privacy and security

When you upload a file to a converter, you want to know what happens to it. DoAide Convert processes files securely and deletes them automatically after conversion. Your files are not stored on any server after the conversion is complete, they are not shared with third parties, and they are not used for training or analysis.
