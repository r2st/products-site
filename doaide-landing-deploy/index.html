Philosophy

How we build

01

Domain-first

We pick one unglamorous domain, learn its regulations and workflows deeply, then build AI that actually understands the constraints. No generic wrappers around a language model.

02

Accuracy over speed

Our models are tuned for precision. A wrong GST filing or a bad 409A valuation costs more than a slow one. We measure ourselves on error rates, not response times.

03

Boring reliability

No flashy demos. Products that work Monday through Friday, handle edge cases, and don't break when the rules change. The best infrastructure is the kind you forget is there.
