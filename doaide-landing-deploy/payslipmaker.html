Everything About Salary Slips in India

What is a Salary Slip?

A salary slip (also called payslip or pay stub) is a document issued by an employer to an employee each month. It details the employee's earnings, deductions, and net pay for that pay period. In India, salary slips are mandatory under the Payment of Wages Act and the Code on Wages 2019.

Components of a Salary Slip

A standard Indian salary slip includes these components:

Earnings

  • Basic Salary: 40-50% of CTC, forms the base for PF, HRA, and gratuity calculations
  • House Rent Allowance (HRA): 40-50% of basic, partially exempt from tax under Old Regime
  • Special Allowance: Remaining amount after allocating basic, HRA, and other components
  • Conveyance Allowance: For commuting expenses, usually ₹1,600/month
  • Medical Allowance: For medical expenses, usually ₹1,250/month

Deductions

  • Employee PF: 12% of basic salary (capped at ₹15,000 basic)
  • ESI: 0.75% of gross salary (for gross ≤ ₹21,000/month)
  • Professional Tax: State-specific, max ₹2,500/year
  • TDS (Income Tax): Based on declared investments and chosen tax regime

Why Salary Slips Matter

Beyond legal compliance, salary slips serve as proof of income for loan applications, rental agreements, visa processing, and income tax filing. Banks require the last 3-6 months of salary slips for home loan and personal loan approvals. A well-formatted salary slip from PaySlipMaker is accepted by all major banks and financial institutions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should a salary slip include?
A salary slip should include: company name and logo, employee name and ID, designation and department, pay period, earnings (basic salary, HRA, special allowance, conveyance, medical), deductions (PF, ESI, professional tax, TDS), and net pay. PaySlipMaker includes all these fields in its templates.
Is a salary slip legally required in India?
Yes. Under the Payment of Wages Act and various state Shops and Establishments Acts, employers are required to provide salary slips to employees. The Code on Wages 2019 mandates that every employer must issue a pay slip to employees before or on the wage period day. Non-compliance can attract penalties.

How is professional tax calculated?
Professional tax rates vary by state. Most states charge ₹200/month (₹2,400/year) for salaried employees above a certain income threshold. Maharashtra charges ₹300/month for salaries above ₹10,000. Delhi does not levy professional tax. PaySlipMaker auto-calculates the correct amount based on the state you select.
What is the difference between CTC and gross salary?
CTC (Cost to Company) is the total amount a company spends on you: gross salary + employer PF + gratuity + insurance + other benefits. Gross salary is what appears on your salary slip before deductions (employee PF, professional tax, TDS). Net salary (take-home) is gross salary minus all employee-side deductions.
