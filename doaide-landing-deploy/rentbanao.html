Why You Need a Rent Agreement

A rent agreement is one of the most important documents for both landlords and tenants in India. Here's everything you need to know about creating one.

Legal Protection

A well-drafted rent agreement protects both parties in case of disputes. It clearly defines the terms of tenancy including rent amount, duration, maintenance responsibilities, and conditions for termination. Without a written agreement, disputes become he-said-she-said situations with no legal recourse.

Required for HRA Claims

If you're a salaried employee claiming House Rent Allowance (HRA) exemption under the Old Tax Regime, you need a rent agreement as proof of rent payment. The Income Tax Department may ask for this document during assessment. For annual rent exceeding ₹1 lakh, the landlord's PAN is also required.

Address Proof & KYC

A registered rent agreement serves as a valid address proof for Aadhaar updates, bank account opening, passport applications, and other KYC requirements. Many institutions specifically ask for a notarized or registered rental agreement.

Stamp Duty by State

Every rent agreement in India must be executed on stamp paper of appropriate value. The stamp duty varies significantly by state:

  • Maharashtra: 0.25% of total rent for the agreement period (min ₹100)
  • Karnataka: 1% of total rent + deposit amount
  • Delhi: ₹100 for agreements up to 5 years
  • Tamil Nadu: 1% of the annual rent
  • Uttar Pradesh: 2% of the annual rent
  • Telangana: 0.4% of total rent for the agreement period

RentBanao automatically calculates the correct stamp duty for your state, so you don't need to look it up.

11-Month vs Longer Agreements

Most rent agreements in India are made for 11 months to avoid mandatory registration. Agreements for 12 months or more must be registered with the Sub-Registrar's office, which involves additional fees (typically 1% of annual rent) and both parties visiting the office. An 11-month agreement with a renewal clause is the most common and cost-effective approach.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a rent agreement mandatory in India?
While not legally mandatory, a rent agreement is strongly recommended. It protects both landlord and tenant, is required for HRA exemption claims, serves as address proof, and is essential for resolving disputes. Most states require registration for agreements exceeding 11 months.
What is the stamp duty on rent agreements?
Stamp duty varies by state. Maharashtra charges 0.25% of the total rent for the agreement period, Karnataka charges 1%, and Delhi charges ₹100 for agreements up to 5 years. RentBanao calculates the exact stamp duty for all 36 states automatically based on your rent amount and agreement duration.
Do I need to register my rent agreement?
Rent agreements for 11 months or less typically do not require registration, though they should be notarized. Agreements exceeding 11 months must be registered with the local Sub-Registrar's office. Registration fees are usually 1% of the annual rent plus a nominal filing fee.
What clauses should a rent agreement include?
A comprehensive rent agreement should include: names and addresses of landlord and tenant, property description and address, monthly rent and security deposit amounts, lease duration and renewal terms, maintenance responsibilities, termination notice period, restrictions on subletting, pet policy, and dispute resolution mechanism.
