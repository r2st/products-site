DoAide Trade

AI-powered analysis for US stock trading.

DoAide Trade is an AI-powered stock trading analysis platform for US equities. It combines real-time market data with technical and fundamental analysis, surfaces trade ideas with reasoning and citations, and tracks portfolios with risk assessment.

Capabilities

What it does.

Market analysis

Real-time data combined with AI-driven technical and fundamental analysis across sectors.

Trade ideas

Actionable ideas with the thesis, entry, target and stop documented — never a signal without a reason.

Portfolio tracking

Positions, P&L and allocation in one view, updated in real time.

Risk assessment

Position sizing, concentration risk and drawdown analysis before the trade, not after.

Earnings analysis

Quarterly results parsed and compared to estimates and guidance, with the delta that matters highlighted.

Watchlist intelligence

Tracked stocks monitored for price, volume and news signals — alerts when something changes.
