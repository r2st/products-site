About

Why you need financial calculators.

Financial decisions are math problems. Whether you are investing through SIPs, taking a home loan, choosing between tax regimes, planning for retirement, or valuing a business for acquisition -- the answer is a number, and getting that number wrong costs real money. DoAide FinCalc puts every calculator you need in one place, powered by AI that does not just compute but recommends.

SIP and mutual fund returns

A SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) is the most popular way Indians invest in mutual funds. DoAide FinCalc computes your future corpus from monthly investments, factoring in compounding frequency, step-up SIPs (increasing your investment annually), and historical return benchmarks for different fund categories. See the difference between investing 10,000 per month at 12% versus 15% over 20 years -- it is lakhs, and knowing the number changes your behaviour.

EMI and loan amortization

The EMI calculator shows your monthly instalment for any loan amount, interest rate, and tenure. But the real value is the amortization schedule -- a month-by-month table showing how much of each EMI goes toward principal versus interest. For a 50-lakh home loan at 8.5% over 20 years, you pay more in interest than principal for the first 12 years. DoAide FinCalc shows this clearly, so you can decide whether to prepay and how much it saves.

Income tax planning

India has two income tax regimes: old (with deductions under 80C, 80D, HRA, NPS) and new (lower slab rates, fewer deductions). The right choice depends on your salary structure and the deductions you can claim. DoAide FinCalc compares both regimes for your specific income, showing the exact tax liability under each, the effective tax rate, and the recommended regime -- not a generic answer, but one tailored to your numbers.

Retirement planning

Retirement planning is a compounding problem with inflation working against you. DoAide FinCalc takes your current age, retirement age, monthly expenses, inflation rate, and expected returns to calculate the corpus you need. It then works backward to tell you the monthly SIP required to get there. The AI layer adjusts recommendations based on your risk profile and how close you are to retirement.

Business valuation models

For founders, CFOs, and investors, DoAide FinCalc includes DCF (Discounted Cash Flow) and comparable multiples valuation models. Input your revenue, growth rate, margins, and discount rate to get a DCF-based enterprise value. Or use comparable company multiples (EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, P/E) to benchmark against peers. These are the same models used by investment banks, made accessible without a spreadsheet.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is a SIP calculator and how does it work?

A SIP calculator estimates the future value of regular monthly investments in mutual funds. Enter your monthly amount, expected return rate, and investment period. DoAide FinCalc compounds monthly and shows total invested versus estimated corpus.

How is EMI calculated for a loan?

EMI is calculated using the standard formula with principal, monthly interest rate, and number of months. DoAide FinCalc also shows the full amortization schedule with principal and interest breakup for each month.

Which tax regime saves more — old or new?

It depends on your deductions. The old regime allows deductions (80C, 80D, HRA, NPS) while the new regime has lower rates with fewer deductions. DoAide FinCalc compares both for your specific income and recommends the better option.

How much corpus do I need for retirement?

Your corpus depends on current age, retirement age, monthly expenses, inflation, and post-retirement returns. DoAide FinCalc calculates the required corpus and the monthly SIP needed to build it.

Is DoAide FinCalc free to use?

Yes. DoAide FinCalc is completely free — no login, no signup. All calculators including SIP, EMI, tax planning, retirement, and business valuation are available without creating an account.
