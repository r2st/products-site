What is the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST) apply to sales within the same state (intra-state). IGST (Integrated GST) applies to sales between different states (inter-state). The total GST rate stays the same — only the split changes.

What are the GST slab rates in India 2026?

India has four main GST slab rates: 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%. Some items are exempt (0%). Special rates include 0.25% (rough diamonds) and 3% (gold, silver). A compensation cess applies to certain luxury goods above 28%.
