What are the GST return filing deadlines for 2026-27?

GSTR-1 is due on the 11th of the following month (quarterly for QRMP: 13th after quarter end). GSTR-3B is due on the 20th of the following month (22nd/24th for quarterly filers). GSTR-9 annual return is due by 31st December. Use DoAide's filing calendar for all dates.

What is the penalty for late GST filing?

Late filing of GSTR-3B attracts Rs 50/day (Rs 20/day for nil return) up to a maximum of Rs 10,000 per return. Additionally, 18% annual interest applies on the tax amount due. Use DoAide's penalty calculator to compute exact late fees.

Can composition scheme dealers file GST online free?

Yes. Composition dealers file CMP-08 quarterly and GSTR-4 annually. DoAide supports both forms with auto-calculation of tax at composition rates (1% for manufacturers, 5% for restaurants, 6% for services). All free.
