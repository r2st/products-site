Mandatory Registration (Regardless of Turnover)

You must register for GST if you make inter-state taxable supply of goods or services, supply through e-commerce platforms, are a casual taxable person, are an input service distributor, are liable for reverse charge mechanism, are an e-commerce operator, or supply online information and database access services from outside India.

Documents Required for GST Registration

PAN Card

PAN of the business entity (company/LLP/partnership) or the proprietor for sole proprietorship. PAN must be active and linked with Aadhaar.

Aadhaar Card

Aadhaar of the authorized signatory and all partners/directors. Required for Aadhaar authentication which speeds up approval to 3 days.

Business Proof

Partnership deed, certificate of incorporation, or registration certificate. For proprietorship, no separate document — PAN and Aadhaar suffice.

Address Proof

Rent agreement with NOC from landlord, or property tax receipt, or utility bill (electricity/water) not older than 2 months.

Bank Statement

First page of bank passbook or a cancelled cheque showing account number, IFSC code, account holder name, and bank branch.

Photographs

Passport-size photographs of all promoters, partners, or the proprietor. JPEG format, less than 100 KB for online upload.

Step-by-Step Registration Process

  1. Visit GST Portal: Go to gst.gov.in and click Services > Registration > New Registration.
  2. Fill Part A: Enter PAN, mobile number, and email. You'll receive OTPs on both — verify them to get a Temporary Reference Number (TRN).
  3. Fill Part B: Log in with TRN and complete the detailed application — business details, promoter/partner info, authorized signatory, business address, goods/services details, and bank account.
  4. Upload documents: Upload all required documents in the prescribed format (PDF/JPEG, size limits specified for each).
  5. Aadhaar authentication: Complete e-KYC via Aadhaar OTP or visit an Aadhaar facilitation center. This speeds up processing.
  6. Verification by officer: The tax officer reviews your application. They may issue a notice (REG-03) requesting clarifications within 7 working days.
  7. GSTIN issued: If everything is in order, you receive your GSTIN and registration certificate (REG-06) within 3-7 working days.

Types of GST Registration

Regular Registration

For businesses exceeding turnover threshold or making inter-state supplies. Allows full ITC claims and no restriction on input credits.

Composition Scheme

For businesses with turnover up to Rs 1.5 crore (goods) or Rs 50 lakh (services). Pay tax at flat rate (1-6%) but cannot claim ITC.

Casual Taxable Person

For businesses making occasional taxable supplies in a state where they have no fixed place of business. Valid for 90 days, extendable.

Non-Resident Taxable Person

For persons outside India making taxable supplies in India. Must register before commencing business. Valid for 90 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who needs GST registration in India?

GST registration is mandatory if your annual turnover exceeds Rs 40 lakh for goods (Rs 20 lakh for special category states) or Rs 20 lakh for services (Rs 10 lakh for special category states). It is also mandatory for inter-state suppliers, e-commerce sellers, casual taxable persons, and input service distributors regardless of turnover.

What documents are required for GST registration?

You need: PAN card of the business/proprietor, Aadhaar card, proof of business registration (partnership deed, incorporation certificate), address proof of business premises (rent agreement or property documents), bank account statement or cancelled cheque, and passport-size photographs of promoters/partners.

How long does GST registration take?

GST registration typically takes 3-7 working days if all documents are in order. Aadhaar-authenticated applications can be processed in 3 working days. The officer may raise queries, which extends the timeline.

What is the GST registration threshold limit for 2026?

For goods: Rs 40 lakh (Rs 20 lakh for special category states). For services: Rs 20 lakh (Rs 10 lakh for special category states). Below these limits, registration is voluntary but recommended for ITC benefits.

How to check GST registration application status?

Visit gst.gov.in and click 'Track Application Status' under Services > Registration. Enter your ARN (Application Reference Number) received after submission. You can also track via the GST portal dashboard after logging in.
