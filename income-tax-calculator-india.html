Frequently Asked Questions

What are the new regime income tax slabs for FY 2026-27?

Under the new regime: Rs 0-4 lakh: Nil, Rs 4-8 lakh: 5%, Rs 8-12 lakh: 10%, Rs 12-16 lakh: 15%, Rs 16-20 lakh: 20%, Rs 20-24 lakh: 25%, Above Rs 24 lakh: 30%. Standard deduction of Rs 75,000. Tax rebate under 87A up to Rs 12 lakh taxable income.

Old regime or new regime — which saves more tax?

It depends on your deductions. If your total deductions (80C, HRA, NPS, home loan interest, medical insurance) exceed approximately Rs 3.75 lakh, the old regime may save more. Use DoAide's comparison tool at tax.doaide.com/old-vs-new for your exact numbers.

What is the Section 87A tax rebate for 2026-27?

Under the new regime, if your total taxable income is up to Rs 12 lakh (after standard deduction), you get a full tax rebate — meaning zero tax. Under the old regime, the rebate applies up to Rs 5 lakh taxable income with a maximum rebate of Rs 12,500.

How to calculate HRA exemption?

HRA exemption is the lowest of: (1) Actual HRA received, (2) 50% of basic salary for metro cities or 40% for non-metro, (3) Rent paid minus 10% of basic salary. Available only under the old regime. Use DoAide's HRA calculator for instant computation.

What is the maximum 80C deduction limit?

Rs 1.5 lakh per financial year. Eligible investments: EPF, PPF, ELSS mutual funds, NSC, 5-year FD, life insurance premium, children's tuition fees, home loan principal. Available only under the old regime.
