Can I claim ITC on a car bought for my sales team?
Generally no. Section 17(5) blocks ITC on motor vehicles seating up to 13 people, with exceptions such as further supply of vehicles, transport of passengers, or driving training.
What's the due date for GSTR-3B this month?
For monthly filers it's the 20th of the following month. QRMP filers fall on the 22nd or 24th depending on state.

Questions, answered

What is DoAide?

DoAide is a business compliance suite for India. It brings together GSTBot for GST, E-Invoice for e-invoices and e-way bills, TaxFile for income tax returns and Comply for tracking regulatory deadlines.

Who is DoAide for?

Small and growing Indian businesses, chartered accountants and tax professionals who manage compliance for themselves or for many clients.

How much does DoAide cost?

Paid plans start at ₹499 per month. Each product also has free tools you can try without paying.

Do I need a separate account for each product?

Each product runs on its own subdomain. Many free tools work without an account; features that save your records need you to sign in.

Where did the other DoAide tools go?

DoAide now focuses on four compliance products. The EMI calculator and Khata digital ledger are still available under More Tools, and older utilities are listed on the free tools page.
