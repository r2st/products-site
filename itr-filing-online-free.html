Frequently Asked Questions

How to file ITR online for free?

Step 1: Calculate your tax using DoAide TaxFile (tax.doaide.com) — use the income tax calculator, HRA calculator, and salary optimizer to compute your exact liability. Step 2: File your return on the Income Tax Portal (incometax.gov.in) which is free for all taxpayers.

Is DoAide TaxFile free?

Yes, 100% free. All 25+ calculators, guides, and tools are free with no signup. No hidden charges, no premium tier. Income tax, HRA, capital gains, NPS, PPF, SIP, salary optimizer — all free.

What is the income tax slab for FY 2026-27?

Under the new regime: 0-4L nil, 4-8L 5%, 8-12L 10%, 12-16L 15%, 16-20L 20%, 20-24L 25%, above 24L 30%. Rebate up to Rs 12L taxable income. Use DoAide's calculator for exact computation.

Old regime or new regime — which is better?

It depends on your deductions. Use DoAide TaxFile's Old vs New Regime tool to compare with your actual numbers. If total deductions exceed Rs 3.75 lakh, old regime may save more.

Do I need a CA to file ITR?

Not always. Salaried individuals with simple returns (ITR-1) can file on incometax.gov.in for free. Use DoAide TaxFile to prepare. For business income or complex returns, a CA helps.
