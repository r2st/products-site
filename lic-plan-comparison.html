How to Choose the Best LIC Plan

Based on Your Age

In your 20s-30s, prioritize term insurance (Tech Term) for high cover at low cost. Add an endowment (Jeevan Anand/Labh) for forced savings. In your 40s-50s, focus on plans with shorter premium paying terms and higher maturity benefits.

Based on Your Goal

Pure protection: LIC Tech Term (Plan 954) — lowest premium, highest cover. Savings + protection: Jeevan Anand (915) or Jeevan Labh (936) — get maturity benefits with life cover. Regular income: Jeevan Umang (945) — annual payouts after premium term. Child's future: Jeevan Tarun (934) — milestone-based payouts.

Based on Your Budget

Start with term insurance for base coverage (as low as Rs 500/month for Rs 50 lakh cover). Add endowment plans based on your savings capacity. Use DoAide's premium calculator to find exact costs for your age and sum assured.

LIC Plan Categories

Term Plans

Pure life cover with no maturity benefit. Lowest premiums for highest coverage. Best choice for primary life insurance. LIC Tech Term is the only online term plan.

Endowment Plans

Savings + protection combined. Get maturity benefit on survival and death benefit to nominees. Includes bonus accumulation. Most popular category by LIC.

Money-Back Plans

Periodic survival benefits during the policy term (every 5 years typically). Provides liquidity while maintaining life cover. Good for planned expenses.

Whole Life Plans

Coverage until age 100 with annual income after premium term. LIC Jeevan Umang is the flagship whole life plan with 8% annual survival benefit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best LIC plan for a 25-year-old in 2026?

For a 25-year-old, LIC Tech Term (Plan 954) is the best for pure life cover at the lowest premium. For savings + protection, LIC New Jeevan Anand (Plan 915) offers lifelong cover with maturity benefits. For wealth creation, LIC Jeevan Labh (Plan 936) provides high bonus rates. Use DoAide's comparison tool to see exact premiums for your age.

What is the difference between endowment and term plans?

Term plans provide only death benefit — if you survive the policy term, you get nothing back. They offer the highest life cover at the lowest premium. Endowment plans provide both death benefit and maturity benefit — you get a lump sum on surviving the policy term. Endowment premiums are 8-10x higher than term plans for the same sum assured.

Which LIC plan is best for tax saving under 80C?

All LIC plans qualify for Section 80C deduction (up to Rs 1.5 lakh). For pure tax saving, Tech Term gives the lowest premium with maximum coverage. For savings + tax benefits, Jeevan Anand or Jeevan Labh are popular. Maturity amount is tax-free under Section 10(10D) if premium is within limits.

Which LIC plan is best for a child's future?

LIC Jeevan Tarun (Plan 934) is designed for children. It provides survival benefits at ages 20, 22, 24, and the remaining at 25 for education and marriage milestones. LIC New Children's Money Back (Plan 932) is another option with periodic payouts.
