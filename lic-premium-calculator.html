Frequently Asked Questions

How to calculate LIC premium online?

Use DoAide InsureKit at insure.doaide.com/premium-calculator. Select the LIC plan, enter your age and sum assured, choose the premium paying term, and get instant premium details including GST. Supports all current LIC plans.

Is the LIC premium calculator free?

Yes, InsureKit's LIC premium calculator is 100% free. No registration, no subscription, no trial period. All calculators are free forever.

Which LIC plan has the lowest premium?

LIC Tech Term (Plan 954) offers the lowest premiums as a pure term plan. For endowment plans, LIC New Jeevan Anand (Plan 915) is popular for its moderate premiums. Use InsureKit's plan comparison to compare.

Can I calculate LIC maturity amount online?

Yes. InsureKit's maturity calculator estimates maturity amounts including bonus projections based on 10-year historical bonus rates. Available for all endowment and money-back plans.

Does the calculator include GST?

Yes. InsureKit shows premium amounts both with and without GST (currently 4.5% for life insurance). You see the exact amount you'll pay including all taxes.
