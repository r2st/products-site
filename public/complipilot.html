CompliPilot

Know where you stand before the auditor tells you.

CompliPilot is an AI compliance management platform. It ships a DPDP Act toolkit, generates the policies an organisation is required to hold, runs a gap analysis against the obligations that actually apply, and assembles the evidence pack an audit will ask for.

Capabilities

What it does.

DPDP Act toolkit

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act obligations mapped to what an organisation must actually do, with the applicable subset determined by how it handles data.

Policy generation

Privacy notices, retention schedules, consent records and internal policies generated against the obligations rather than downloaded and search-replaced.

Gap analysis

Current state assessed against required state, with the gaps ranked by exposure so the list is a plan and not an inventory.

Audit preparation

The evidence an audit will request, assembled and organised, with the missing items named while there is still time to produce them.

Obligation tracking

Recurring requirements — reviews, renewals, attestations — on a calendar that raises them before they lapse.

Change monitoring

When an obligation shifts, the policies and controls it touches are flagged rather than left quietly out of date.

Workflow

How it works.

Step 01

Describe the organisation

What data is handled, for whom, and where it goes. The applicable obligations follow from that.

Step 02

Run the gap analysis

Current state against required state, with gaps ranked by exposure.

Step 03

Generate the policies

The documents the obligations require, drafted against your actual practices.

Step 04

Prepare for audit

Evidence assembled, gaps closed, and the recurring obligations put on a calendar.

Detail

What’s inside.

Obligation mapping — statutory requirements resolved to the subset that applies to you
Ranked gap register — findings ordered by exposure rather than listed alphabetically
Generated policy set — notices, retention schedules and internal policies drafted to fit
Evidence assembly — the artefacts an audit requests, collected and organised in advance
Recurring obligations — reviews, renewals and attestations tracked on a calendar
Change alerts — affected policies flagged when an underlying obligation moves
