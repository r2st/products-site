Documedic

Clinical decision support that shows its working.

Documedic is an AI clinical decision support system, built around the Aether Clinician engine. It produces evidence-based recommendations, surfaces drug interactions, and works through a differential — always with the reasoning visible and the source cited, because a recommendation a clinician cannot audit is a recommendation they cannot use.

Capabilities

What it does.

Evidence-based recommendations

Suggestions grounded in the published evidence, with the strength of that evidence stated rather than implied — so a strong recommendation and a weak one do not arrive looking identical.

Drug interaction checking

The active medication list checked for interactions, contraindications and dose concerns, with the mechanism explained and the severity graded.

Differential diagnosis

A ranked differential from the presenting picture, with the discriminating findings for each entry and what would move it up or down the list.

Transparent reasoning

Every output shows the path to it. The clinician evaluates the argument, not just the conclusion.

Citation trail

Each recommendation carries its sources. Follow them; the system is designed to be checked.

Clinician in command

Documedic supports a decision. It does not make one, and it does not present itself as having made one.

Workflow

How it works.

Step 01

Enter the presentation

History, findings, current medications and relevant results.

Step 02

Review the differential

A ranked list with discriminating features and the evidence behind each position.

Step 03

Check the interactions

Medication conflicts and contraindications surfaced with mechanism and severity.

Step 04

Audit the reasoning

Open the working behind any recommendation and follow the citations to the source.

Detail

What’s inside.

Ranked differential — ordered by fit with the discriminating findings stated per entry
Severity-graded interactions — conflicts classified rather than listed flat
Evidence strength labels — the quality of the underlying evidence stated on every recommendation
Reasoning traces — the full path from presentation to suggestion, open for inspection
Source citations — references attached and followable on each output
Decision support, not decision — the clinician remains the decision-maker throughout
