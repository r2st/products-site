Part A vs Part B

Part A captures supplier, recipient, and goods details. Part B captures vehicle number and transporter details. Part B is required for road transport over 50 km.

🔄

Extension & Cancellation

E-way bills can be extended before expiry if goods can't reach destination in time. They can be cancelled within 24 hours if goods are not transported or invoice is cancelled.

📱

Generation Methods

Generate e-way bills via the EWB portal (ewaybillgst.gov.in), SMS, Android app, API integration, or through GST Suvidha Providers (GSPs).

Frequently Asked Questions

An e-way bill is required for inter-state movement of goods when the consignment value exceeds ₹50,000. For intra-state movement, the threshold is generally ₹50,000, though some states have different limits (e.g., ₹1 lakh in some states for certain goods). The bill must be generated before the movement of goods begins.
For regular cargo: 1 day for the first 200 km, plus 1 additional day for every 200 km or part thereof. For over-dimensional cargo: 1 day for the first 20 km, plus 1 day for every 20 km. Validity starts from the time of generation and can be extended before expiry.
Under Section 129 of the CGST Act, if goods are transported without a valid e-way bill, the penalty is the tax amount or ₹10,000, whichever is higher. Additionally, the goods and vehicle can be detained or seized. The penalty applies to the consignor, consignee, or transporter.
Exempt goods include: goods exempted under GST notifications, goods by non-motorized transport, transit cargo from customs, LPG for household use, kerosene under PDS, postal department goods, precious metals (gold, silver), used personal effects, empty cargo containers, and goods specifically listed in Annexure to Rule 138(14).
