About

Why Indian businesses need a free GST calculator online.

India's Goods and Services Tax replaced a patchwork of indirect taxes in 2017, but the compliance burden it places on small and medium businesses has only grown since. Every invoice needs the correct HSN or SAC code, the right tax rate split between CGST, SGST, and IGST, and the transaction must reconcile against what the counterparty files. Miss a deadline or file a mismatch and the penalties arrive faster than the refund ever will.

What a GST calculator actually needs to do

A basic GST calculator tells you how much tax to add to an amount. That is useful for about thirty seconds of your day. What a business actually needs is a GST compliance tool that handles the full lifecycle: calculating the tax split based on transaction type and location, finding the correct HSN code for every line item, tracking which invoices have been filed and which are still outstanding, and making sure the Input Tax Credit you are about to claim will actually survive reconciliation against the supplier's GSTR-2A data.

How DoAide GST works as a free GST filing software

DoAide GST is not a spreadsheet with a tax formula. It is a GST compliance tool free for Indian businesses that reads your invoices — from PDFs, scanned images, photographs, and email attachments — and extracts the GSTIN, HSN code, taxable value, applicable rate, and tax split automatically. Every parsed invoice is indexed, searchable, and reconciled against the data available on the GST portal. When a purchase invoice does not match what the supplier declared, the mismatch surfaces immediately rather than at filing time.

HSN code finder built into every calculation

One of the most common compliance errors is using the wrong HSN or SAC code. DoAide GST includes a built-in HSN code finder that searches across the full 8-digit classification system. Type a product description and see the matching codes with their applicable GST rates. The code is then carried through to invoice parsing, return preparation, and reconciliation — no manual lookup tables or guesswork required.

GSTR filing and Input Tax Credit tracking

DoAide GST prepares GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B returns from your invoice data. The filing calendar tracks every return your business is liable for, with escalating reminders as deadlines approach. For Input Tax Credit, the tool matches your purchase invoices against supplier-filed data so you can see exactly which credits are safe to claim and which are at risk before you file. This prevents the common scenario where ITC is claimed, questioned during assessment, and reversed with interest.

Who should use DoAide GST

DoAide GST is built for Indian small and medium businesses, solo proprietors, freelancers who need to issue GST invoices, chartered accountant firms managing multiple clients, and anyone who files GST returns and wants to stop doing it from a spreadsheet. The tool is free to use — there is no premium tier for the core GST calculator, HSN code lookup, or compliance calendar features. If you are searching for a free GST calculator online India or GST filing software that does not charge per return, DoAide GST is built for exactly that.

Beyond the calculator: a complete GST compliance tool

Most free GST calculators stop at arithmetic. DoAide GST continues into the territory that actually matters: mismatch detection, ITC reconciliation, deadline management, and return preparation. Every invoice is retained and searchable, so when an assessment notice asks about a transaction from three quarters ago, the answer is a search query rather than an afternoon of digging through files. The goal is not to replace your CA — it is to hand them clean, reconciled data instead of a box of invoices.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is DoAide GST calculator really free?

Yes. The GST calculator, HSN code finder, and compliance calendar are completely free. There is no signup required for basic calculations, and no premium tier that locks core features behind a paywall. DoAide GST is built as a free GST compliance tool for Indian businesses of every size.

How do I find the correct HSN code for my product?

Use the built-in HSN code finder. Type your product or service description and DoAide GST searches across all 8-digit HSN and SAC codes to find the correct classification. It also shows the applicable GST rate for each code, so you can verify you are charging the right amount.

Can DoAide GST help with GSTR filing?

Yes. DoAide GST auto-prepares GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B returns from your invoice data. It reconciles purchase records against supplier filings, flags mismatches, and assembles the figures needed before each deadline. You review and submit — the data is already organized.

What is Input Tax Credit and how does DoAide track it?

Input Tax Credit (ITC) is the GST you paid on business purchases that can be offset against the GST you collect on sales. DoAide matches your purchase invoices against supplier-declared data on the GST portal, showing which credits are safe to claim and which are at risk of being reversed during assessment.

Is my data safe with DoAide GST?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. DoAide does not share your financial data with third parties. Invoice data and GSTIN information are stored securely and accessible only to your account. The platform follows industry-standard security practices for handling sensitive financial information.
