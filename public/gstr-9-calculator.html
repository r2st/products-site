What is GSTR-9 and Why You Need This Calculator

GSTR-9 is the annual return that every registered GST taxpayer in India must file. It consolidates all the monthly or quarterly returns -- GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, and amendments -- filed during a financial year into a single comprehensive summary. The form captures details of outward supplies, inward supplies, Input Tax Credit availed and reversed, tax paid, and any demands or refunds during the year. Filing GSTR-9 accurately is essential for GST compliance and avoiding notices from the tax department.

This free GSTR-9 annual return calculator helps businesses and tax professionals prepare their annual return by performing four critical calculations: turnover reconciliation, tax liability comparison, ITC reconciliation, and filing status determination. Instead of manually cross-checking figures across multiple returns and ledgers, this tool gives you a structured summary that highlights mismatches before you file.

Turnover Reconciliation for GSTR-9

One of the most common reasons for GST notices is a mismatch between turnover declared in GSTR-9 and the turnover in financial statements. The turnover reconciliation section lets you enter your total sales as per books, exports, exempt supplies, non-GST supplies, and taxable supplies separately. The calculator then computes the aggregate turnover and compares the sum of individual components with your total sales figure. Any difference between these two numbers needs to be investigated -- it could be due to credit notes, debit notes, advances, or supplies that were missed in monthly returns.

Tax Liability and Payment Reconciliation

The tax liability section of GSTR-9 requires you to declare the total tax collected under IGST, CGST, SGST, and Cess, and then show how it was paid -- either through Input Tax Credit utilisation or cash payment through the Electronic Cash Ledger. Our calculator adds up both payment methods and compares the total tax paid against the total liability. If there is a shortfall, it means you have unpaid tax that must be settled before filing. If there is an excess, it may indicate over-payment or errors in return filing that need correction.

ITC Reconciliation: GSTR-3B vs GSTR-2B

Input Tax Credit reconciliation is arguably the most complex and error-prone part of GSTR-9. The ITC you claimed in your monthly GSTR-3B returns may not match the ITC reflected in your GSTR-2B auto-generated statements. Common reasons include invoices uploaded late by suppliers, credit notes not accounted for, or ITC claimed on ineligible supplies. This calculator compares IGST, CGST, and SGST separately between GSTR-3B and GSTR-2B, accounts for ITC reversed during the year, and shows you the net ITC available along with any mismatch. An excess claim over GSTR-2B is flagged as it could trigger a notice under Section 16(2)(aa).

GSTR-9 Filing Thresholds

Not every GST-registered business needs to file GSTR-9. As per the latest notifications, GSTR-9 is mandatory only for taxpayers whose aggregate turnover exceeds Rs 2 crore in the financial year. For businesses below this threshold, filing is optional. Additionally, taxpayers with aggregate turnover exceeding Rs 5 crore must also file GSTR-9C, which is a self-certified reconciliation statement between the GSTR-9 figures and the audited annual financial statements. The filing status section of this calculator instantly tells you where you stand based on your turnover.

Important GSTR-9 Due Dates

The due date for filing GSTR-9 is December 31 of the year following the financial year. For FY 2025-26, the due date is December 31, 2026. Late filing attracts a late fee of Rs 200 per day (Rs 100 CGST + Rs 100 SGST), subject to a maximum of 0.5% of the turnover in the state or union territory. Filing on time also avoids the risk of best-judgment assessments by the department under Section 62.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is GSTR-9?

GSTR-9 is the annual return filed by registered GST taxpayers. It consolidates all monthly/quarterly returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B) into a single summary covering outward supplies, inward supplies, ITC claimed, tax paid, and amendments for the entire financial year.

Who needs to file GSTR-9?

GSTR-9 is mandatory for taxpayers with aggregate turnover exceeding Rs 2 crore. It is optional for those below this threshold. Composition dealers, casual taxable persons, input service distributors, non-resident taxable persons, and TDS/TCS deductors are exempt from GSTR-9.

What is GSTR-9C and when is it required?

GSTR-9C is a self-certified reconciliation statement filed along with GSTR-9 by taxpayers whose aggregate turnover exceeds Rs 5 crore. It reconciles GSTR-9 values with audited financial statements. From FY 2020-21 onwards, CA certification is no longer required -- it is self-certified by the taxpayer.

What is the due date for GSTR-9 filing?

The due date for GSTR-9 is December 31 of the year following the financial year. For FY 2025-26, GSTR-9 is due by December 31, 2026. Late filing attracts a fee of Rs 200/day (Rs 100 CGST + Rs 100 SGST), capped at 0.5% of turnover in the state/UT.
