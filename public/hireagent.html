HireAgent

The hiring pipeline, end to end, with the paperwork included.

HireAgent is an AI hiring and recruitment platform covering the stretch TalentPing hands off: assessing candidates against the role, tracking the pipeline as a measurable process, and generating the offer letter when it is time to send one.

Capabilities

What it does.

Skill assessments

Assessments generated for the specific role rather than pulled from a library, scored against the requirement with the reasoning shown.

Offer letter generation

Offers assembled from the role, the agreed terms and the company template — correct on the first pass, not the third.

Pipeline analytics

Stage-by-stage conversion, time in stage, and where candidates drop. A funnel with a diagnosis, not just numbers.

Outreach sequences

Candidate messaging across the process: invitations, reminders, status updates, and the messages nobody remembers to send.

Structured scoring

Interview feedback captured against defined criteria so decisions compare across candidates and across interviewers.

Process visibility

Every open role and every candidate in one view, with what is blocked and who is blocking it.

Workflow

How it works.

Step 01

Define the role

Requirements, assessment criteria and the stages a candidate moves through.

Step 02

Assess

Role-specific assessments issued and scored, with the reasoning behind each score visible.

Step 03

Track the pipeline

Candidates move through stages with conversion and time-in-stage measured as they go.

Step 04

Make the offer

Terms agreed, letter generated from the template, sent and tracked to signature.

Detail

What’s inside.

Role-specific assessments — generated per requirement rather than drawn from a fixed bank
Structured scorecards — interview feedback captured against defined, comparable criteria
Stage conversion metrics — drop-off measured per stage and per source
Offer templating — company terms and role specifics merged into a correct first draft
Candidate comms — invitations, reminders and status updates sent automatically
Time-in-stage tracking — the stalls made visible while they can still be fixed
