About

Why startups need 409A valuation software.

Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code requires that stock options granted to employees be priced at or above fair market value. If the exercise price is set below FMV, the options are treated as deferred compensation, and the employee faces a 20% penalty tax plus interest on vesting. A 409A valuation establishes that FMV with a defensible, independent analysis — it is not optional if you are issuing equity to employees.

The problem with traditional 409A valuations

Traditional valuation firms charge between $5,000 and $15,000 per report and take two to four weeks to deliver. For an early-stage startup that needs to move fast and conserve cash, this creates a real tension: you need the valuation before you can grant options to new hires, but the cost and timeline of getting one can delay the hiring process itself. Many startups delay their 409A, granting options informally and hoping to backdate — a practice that creates exactly the kind of risk Section 409A was designed to prevent.

How DoAide makes 409A valuations affordable

DoAide 409A uses AI to run the same valuation methodologies that traditional firms use — discounted cash flow analysis, guideline public company method, and option pricing / backsolve models — but without the manual analyst time that drives up the cost. You upload your cap table, recent financials or projections, and details of any funding rounds. The AI engine processes the inputs, runs the models, and produces a board-ready report in 24 hours. The result is a cheap 409A valuation that does not compromise on the analytical rigor needed for IRS compliance.

When you need a 409A valuation

You need a new 409A valuation before your first stock option grant, after any priced equity round (the previous valuation is immediately stale), when a material event changes company value (a large customer win, a pivot, an acquisition offer), and at least once every 12 months to maintain safe harbor protection. Most startups between seed and Series C will need three to five valuations in their first few years. At traditional pricing, that is $15,000 to $75,000 spent on a compliance document. With DoAide 409A valuation software, the same coverage costs a fraction of that.

IRS compliance and audit defense

DoAide 409A reports follow IRS Revenue Ruling 59-60 and are structured to provide safe harbor protection under Section 409A. Each report documents the methodologies applied, the assumptions used, and the conclusion reached with enough detail that an auditor can follow the reasoning. The report is not a shortcut — it is a defensible valuation that happens to be produced faster and cheaper than the traditional alternative because the analytical work is done by software rather than junior analysts billing by the hour.

Who uses DoAide 409A

DoAide 409A is used by startup founders and CFOs who need a valuation before granting equity, startup lawyers who advise clients on option pricing, and accelerators that need valuations for portfolio companies. If you are searching for a startup 409A valuation that does not cost $10,000 and does not take a month, or for cheap 409A valuation software that still meets IRS standards, DoAide 409A is purpose-built for that exact need.
