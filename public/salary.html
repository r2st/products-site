About

Why you need a salary calculator India.

In India, your CTC (Cost to Company) and your take-home salary are very different numbers. Between employer PF contributions, employee PF deductions, professional tax, income tax, HRA exemptions, and the choice between old and new tax regimes, figuring out what you actually receive each month requires serious arithmetic. A CTC to in-hand salary calculator removes the guesswork — enter your CTC, and see exactly what lands in your bank account.

CTC to in-hand salary breakdown

DoAide Salary takes your annual CTC and breaks it into every component: basic salary, HRA, special allowances, employer PF, employee PF, professional tax, and income tax under both regimes. You see the monthly and annual figures side by side. For someone with a 12 LPA CTC, the difference between old and new regime can be over ₹30,000 per year — knowing which one is better for your specific situation directly affects your finances.

Old vs new tax regime comparison

India offers two income tax regimes. The old regime has higher tax rates but allows deductions under Sections 80C, 80D, 80CCD, HRA exemption, and LTA. The new regime has lower slab rates (revised in Budget 2024) but removes most deductions. The right choice depends entirely on your salary structure and the deductions you can claim. DoAide Salary compares both regimes for your specific CTC and deductions, showing you which one puts more money in your pocket.

PF, HRA, and deduction calculations

Employee Provident Fund (PF) is calculated at 12% of basic salary, but many employers cap the basic at ₹15,000/month. HRA exemption depends on actual HRA received, rent paid, salary, and whether you live in a metro city. DoAide Salary handles these calculations with proper rounding and capping, so the take-home number you see matches what actually appears on your payslip.

Salary benchmarking tool

Knowing your take-home is half the picture. DoAide Salary also includes a salary benchmarking tool that shows you how your compensation compares to market rates for the same role, experience level, and city. Whether you are negotiating a raise, evaluating a job offer, or setting salaries for your team, the benchmarking data gives you a realistic range based on aggregated, anonymized salary data across Indian companies.

Who should use DoAide Salary

DoAide Salary is for anyone in India who wants to understand their compensation: employees evaluating a new offer, job seekers comparing packages, HR teams building compensation structures, and founders setting salaries. If you have ever wondered "What is my actual take-home from this CTC?" or "Am I being paid fairly for my role?" — this is the tool that answers both questions.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is the difference between CTC and in-hand salary?

CTC is the total cost your employer spends on you, including base salary, allowances, PF employer contribution, insurance, and bonuses. In-hand salary is what you receive after PF, professional tax, and income tax deductions.

Which tax regime should I choose — old or new?

It depends on your deductions. The old regime has higher rates but allows deductions (80C, HRA, NPS). The new regime has lower slab rates with fewer deductions. DoAide Salary compares both for your specific case.

How is PF calculated from CTC?

Employee PF is 12% of basic salary (capped at ₹15,000/month basic for some employers). The employer matches 12%, split between EPF (3.67%) and EPS (8.33%). DoAide Salary shows the exact breakup.

How accurate is the salary benchmarking?

Benchmarks are based on aggregated, anonymized salary data across Indian companies, refreshed regularly and filtered by role, experience level, and city. It gives a realistic range for negotiations.

Yes. DoAide Salary is completely free — no login, no signup. Calculate CTC to in-hand salary, compare tax regimes, and benchmark salaries without creating an account.
