VoiceDesk

Every call routed, transcribed and understood.

VoiceDesk is an AI voice and call management platform. It routes inbound calls on detected intent rather than a menu tree, transcribes everything, and turns the resulting record into analytics that describe what callers are actually ringing about.

Capabilities

What it does.

Intent-based routing

The caller says why they are ringing and the call goes to the right place. No menu tree, no pressing three to hear the options again.

Real-time transcription

Every call transcribed as it happens, speaker-separated, searchable the moment it ends.

Caller intent detection

Intent classified on connect and carried with the call, so whoever picks up already knows what it is about.

Call analytics

Volume, wait time, resolution rate and — more usefully — what callers are actually calling about, by theme, over time.

Queue management

Live queue state with routing rules that respond to load instead of holding people in a line nobody is watching.

Post-call summaries

A written summary and the follow-up actions, generated from the transcript rather than typed by whoever took the call.

Workflow

How it works.

Step 01

Connect the number

Point the inbound line at VoiceDesk and define the destinations calls can route to.

Step 02

Describe the intents

The reasons people call and where each should go. Routing follows from that.

Step 03

Take the calls

Intent detected on connect, call routed, transcript produced live.

Step 04

Read the pattern

Analytics on what callers want, where calls stall, and which intents are growing.

Detail

What’s inside.

Speaker-separated transcripts — each side of the call attributed and timestamped
Intent taxonomy — call reasons defined once and applied to routing and reporting alike
Live queue state — wait times and load visible while the queue is running
Post-call summaries — written outcome and follow-up actions generated from the transcript
Searchable call archive — every call queryable by caller, intent or phrase
Trend analytics — call themes tracked over time, not just counted
