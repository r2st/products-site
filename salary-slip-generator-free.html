What’s Included in Your Salary Slip

Basic Salary + DA

Core salary component and dearness allowance. Typically 40-50% of CTC. Forms the basis for PF, gratuity, and HRA calculations.

House Rent Allowance

HRA component with automatic exemption calculation based on city category (metro/non-metro), rent paid, and basic salary.

Special Allowance

Flexible component covering conveyance, medical, LTA, and other allowances. Fully taxable unless specific exemptions claimed.

PF Deduction (12%)

Employee's contribution to Provident Fund — 12% of basic + DA. Employer's matching contribution shown separately. Tax-deductible under 80C.

Professional Tax

State-level professional tax deduction. Varies by state — Rs 200/month in most states, Rs 300 for Maharashtra (Feb), Rs 150 for others.

TDS / Income Tax

Tax deducted at source based on employee's declared investments and tax regime choice. Auto-calculated per income tax slabs.

What details are included in a salary slip?

A salary slip includes employee name, ID, designation, department, PAN, and bank account. Earnings: basic, HRA, DA, special allowance, conveyance, medical. Deductions: PF (12%), ESI (0.75%), professional tax, TDS, loan recovery. Net pay = gross earnings minus total deductions.
