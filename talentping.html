TalentPing

Sourcing, outreach and prep — one loop, run continuously.

TalentPing is an AI talent sourcing platform. It discovers candidates against a role rather than a keyword, runs the outreach sequence, and produces a prep briefing for every conversation that comes back — so the pipeline moves without a recruiter driving each step by hand.

Capabilities

What it does.

Candidate discovery

Describe the role in prose. TalentPing builds the search from the description — adjacent titles, equivalent stacks, the companies that produce this profile — instead of matching the words you happened to type.

Match reasoning

Every candidate arrives with the case for them written out: what lines up, what does not, and which part of the requirement is being stretched.

Outreach sequences

First-touch and follow-ups written per candidate against the specific reason they are a fit, then sent on a schedule that does not read as a blast.

Prep briefings

Before a call, a briefing: background, likely motivations, the gaps worth probing, and the questions that will actually tell you something.

Pipeline view

Where every candidate stands across roles, what is stalled, and which conversations need a human this week.

Role calibration

Rejections feed back into the search. The tenth batch of candidates is measurably better targeted than the first.

Workflow

How it works.

Step 01

Write the role

A description in plain prose. TalentPing derives the search criteria, including the ones you would not have thought to specify.

Step 02

Review the matches

Candidates ranked with the reasoning attached. Reject freely — each rejection sharpens the next batch.

Step 03

Launch outreach

Sequences written per candidate and sent on a paced schedule, with replies classified as they arrive.

Step 04

Take the call prepared

A briefing lands ahead of each conversation with the background and the questions worth asking.

Detail

What’s inside.

Semantic role matching — search built from the role description, not from keyword overlap
Adjacent-title expansion — equivalent roles surfaced under the names other companies use
Per-candidate messaging — outreach written against the specific reason for the match
Reply classification — interested, not now, referral, decline — routed automatically
Interview briefings — background, motivations and probing questions per candidate
Feedback learning — accept and reject signals recalibrate subsequent searches
